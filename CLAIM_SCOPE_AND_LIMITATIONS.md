# Claim scope

Both problems are NL-complete under deterministic logspace many-one reductions in every fixed positive dimension. Updates are integers, endpoint coordinates are rational with unary numerator and denominator magnitudes, and all intermediate counter values may be signed.

The argument uses fixed dimension, unary magnitudes, integer updates and arbitrary signed rational intermediate counter values. Nonnegative or tested prefixes, rational edge weights, variable dimension and binary magnitudes are outside its scope.

The release contains an analytic mathematical argument, a finite exact replay of selected algebraic steps, and an illustrative rotating figure. No proof-assistant formalization is included. The manuscript has not been peer reviewed.
