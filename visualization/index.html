A short run on one rational grid

Two loop firings, followed by one capped bridge

  • Loop at p · multiplier 1 then 1/2
  • Bridge to q · multiplier 1/2
  • Common denominator 2

The run starts at p(0,0). Two occurrences of the (2,0) loop give p(2,0) and p(3,0). The (-1,3) bridge then gives q(5/2,3/2). Height shows the step index, and horizontal coordinates give the two counters. Every displayed point is an exact half-grid point.
